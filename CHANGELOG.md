# FermataX Changelog

## [2.1] - 2026-10-29

### What's New & Noteworthy
- **Glass theme (New)**: A frosted-glass look for Android Auto and phones, with a GPS speedometer, trip computer, hourly weather, custom shortcuts and its own Day/Night switch. Features translucent acrylic cards, responsive Bento dashboard layouts, and seamless theme switching.
- **Use your phone as a remote ("Open on Car")**: Skip tracks, Play/Pause, or pick content on your phone and send it to the car screen. Essential when the car display has no touch screen.
- **Car Finder & Radar**: A map, compass radar and a photo of your parking spot help you find your car in big parking lots, with full Day and Night mode support.
- **Smoother YouTube & Stremio**: More accurate seeking, fullscreen, steering wheel buttons and SponsorBlock, plus a better ad blocker. Stremio on Android Auto now has a dedicated on-screen Back button that takes you back to FermataX (both Classic and Glass themes).
- **More stable Android Auto**: Fewer crashes and smoother cable plug/unplug during playback. Video keeps playing when Maps covers the screen. Support for 9 languages: English, Vietnamese, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, Traditional Chinese, Turkish, German, and Italian.
- **Easier setup & support**: All permissions in one checklist, brand-specific tips to keep the app running in the background, and a diagnostics export for support (no location or personal data).
- **Trip computer that keeps counting**: Allow location "All the time" to keep speed, distance and trip time updating while Google Maps covers the car screen or the phone is locked, and to start tracking as soon as the car connects. With "While using the app", some phones pause location in the background; the speed card then shows "—" and explains why.
- **More reliable speed and clearer location status**: The speed card prefers GPS, then Google fused location, then the system fused provider. If location stays unavailable for more than 8 seconds, the card clears the old speed and explains when background access is needed.

### Streamlined & Optimized
- **Removed bulky features**: ChatGPT, Google Drive, Chromecast, and offline translation removed — the app is now significantly lighter, cleaner and starts faster.
- **Consolidated Themes & Languages**: 3 refined interfaces (Glassmorphism, Material You & Classic) and 9 languages.
