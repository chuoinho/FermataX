# Asset Origins

- `fermatax-logo.png`: FermataX application artwork from this repository.
- `showcase.mp4`: Generated locally from FermataX branding by `promo/tv-demo/build-demo.ps1`.
- `feature-highlights.mp4`: Generated locally from FermataX text and branding by `promo/tv-demo/build-demo.ps1`.
- `scenic-motion-dhu.mp4`: Direct capture of FermataX playing the original Scenic Drive demo channel on Android Auto DHU.
- Dashboard, channel-list and split-view PNGs: Direct Android Auto DHU captures of FermataX.
- Final ambient audio: Synthesized locally by FFmpeg; no sampled or third-party music is used.

YouTube and Stremio appear only as unmodified add-on identifiers in FermataX Dashboard captures. No YouTube or Stremio media content is shown.
